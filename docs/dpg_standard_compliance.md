## 1. Executive Summary

**AgriStack DBT Core** is an open-source, Zero-Trust Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) orchestration and governance engine built on Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) principles. It connects multi-channel citizen entry points (IVR Voice, WhatsApp Messaging, USSD Menu Signaling, SMS) with sovereign AI language blocks for intent and entity extraction, cross-matches claims against federated agricultural land registries, enforces deterministic 3-Gate governance routing with 0% generative AI involvement in financial eligibility, and interfaces with sovereign clearing rails under strict Zero-PII guarantees.

This document maps the implemented safeguards, database schemas, API endpoints, and verification artifacts of **AgriStack DBT Core** against all nine indicators of the **Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA) Standard**.

## 3. Statutory Privacy, Consent & Data Minimization Policy (Indicator 7 & 9A)

**AgriStack DBT Core** is engineered to comply by default with **India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023**, the **Information Technology Act 2000 ( and Aadhaar Data Vault guidelines)**, and **GDPR Article 25 (Data Protection by Design and by Default)**:

1. **PII Collection & Non-Storage Classification (DPGA 9A):**
   * *DPGA Classification:* **"PII data can be collected but NOT stored and NOT distributed."**
   * Raw citizen identifiers (caller CLI/mobile number, Aadhaar number, or Farmer ID) enter only the ephemeral Layer 2 Perimeter Security Gateway in volatile RAM and are immediately transformed into a 64-character hexadecimal SHA-256 HMAC token using an environment-isolated cryptographic salt.
2. **Free-Text PII Scrubbing & Database Enforcement:**
   * Before any voice transcript or chat message leaves Layer 2, the in-memory PII Purge Sentinel redacts 12-digit Aadhaar patterns, 10-digit mobile numbers, and personal identifiers.
   * Relational tables (`session_telemetry_log` and `application_governance_ledger`) enforce `CHECK (length(tokenized_phone_hash) = 64)` and `CHECK (length(tokenized_identity_hash) = 64)` constraints so unhashed identifiers cannot be persisted.
3. **Purpose Limitation & Consent Provenance:**
   * Tokenized claims are processed solely for agricultural subsidy and disaster-relief adjudication under explicit session consent schemas defined in [schema.sql](../design-specs/step2-data-schemas/schema.sql).

## 4. Content Moderation, Anti-Harassment & Underage Protection Policy (Indicator 9B & 9C)

1. **Handling Inappropriate, Misleading & Illegal Content (DPGA 9B):**
   * Inbound citizen audio transcripts and text messages are synchronously inspected by the Layer 2 Adversarial Prompt Firewall (< 5 ms latency) prior to entity extraction.
   * Inputs containing prompt-injection directives, role overrides, SQL/command injection patterns, or abusive/manipulative payloads are immediately flagged (`is_adversarial_attempt = True`) and deterministically rejected at Gate 1 (`AUTO_REJECT` with `ADVERSARIAL_PROMPT_DETECTED`) without invoking financial workflows.
2. **Protection from Harassment & Underage Safety (DPGA 9C):**
   * **Zero Peer-to-Peer Surface:** Citizens interact exclusively with a constrained, deterministic state workflow; users cannot view, message, or interact with other citizens.
   * **Underage User Safeguards:** Eligibility verification requires a verified adult Farmer ID / cadastral land parcel match in the Federated Farmer Registry and an adult Aadhaar-seeded DBT bank account.
   * **Contributor & Operator Protection:** All repository contributors, reviewers, and integrators are governed by [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and confidential reporting channels in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
